What's inside

Vouchers

Payment, receipt, contra, journal, sales, purchase, credit and debit notes, orders and stock vouchers with F4–F9 shortcuts.

GST

CGST/SGST/IGST invoices, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B reconciliation, HSN summary, e-invoice and e-way bill JSON.

Inventory

Stock items, godowns, batches, price lists, FIFO/LIFO/average costing, stock and movement reports.

Reports

Balance Sheet, P&L, Trial Balance, Day Book, Cash Flow, Funds Flow, ratios, registers, outstandings.

Payroll & TDS

Employees, pay heads, PF/ESI/PT, pay slips, TDS/TCS deductions, challans and Form 26Q summary.

Banking

Bank reconciliation with statement import, cheque register and printing, post-dated cheques.

Safe data

Data stays on your computer. Backup and restore, edit log, users, roles and passwords.

Free updates

The app checks for new versions and updates itself in place.

Switching from Tally

1In Tally, export your company masters and vouchers as XML.
2In TallyBook press Alt+O (Import), choose Masters, then Transactions, and select the files.
3Review the preview. Nothing is written until you accept, and importing twice never duplicates.

Excel and CSV imports use Tally's sample templates.

Installing

macOSOpen the .dmg and drag TallyBook to Applications. Early builds are not notarised yet: the first time, right-click the app and choose Open.
WindowsRun the .msi or -setup.exe. If SmartScreen warns, choose More info, then Run anyway.
LinuxUse the .AppImage (make it executable), or install the .deb / .rpm.

Why businesses switch to TallyBook

₹0, every year

No licence fee and no yearly renewal. Every feature is included.

Works on Mac and Linux

Runs natively on Windows, Mac (including M1–M4) and Linux. No virtual machine needed.

Same keyboard workflow

F4–F9 vouchers, Alt+G Go To, Ctrl+A accept, Gateway menu. No retraining.

Bring your Tally data

Everything a small business needs

Vouchers

Payment, receipt, contra, journal, sales, purchase, credit and debit notes, orders, delivery and receipt notes, stock journal.

GST

CGST/SGST/IGST invoices, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B reconciliation, HSN summary, e-invoice and e-way bill JSON.

Inventory

Stock items, godowns, batches, price levels, FIFO, LIFO, average and standard costing, stock and movement reports.

Reports

Balance Sheet, P&L, Trial Balance, Day Book, ledgers, registers, cash flow, funds flow, ratios, outstandings.

Payroll

Employees, pay heads, attendance, PF, ESI and professional tax, payroll vouchers and pay slips.

TDS and TCS

Nature of payment, deduction during entry, TDS outstandings, challan reconciliation, Form 26Q summary.

Banking

Bank reconciliation with statement import, cheque register and printing, post-dated cheques.

Safe and private

Offline by default. Backup and restore, edit log, users, roles and passwords.

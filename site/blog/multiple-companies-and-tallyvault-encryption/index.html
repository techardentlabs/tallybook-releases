Multiple companies and TallyVault encryption in TallyBook

Techardent Labs · 3 November 2026 · 5 min read
Quick answer: Each business can be its own company with its own data file. Press Alt+F3 → Create to add one, F3 to change company and Ctrl+F3 to shut it. Set a TallyVault password under Alt+K → Company Security to encrypt that company's data and backups. A lost TallyVault password cannot be recovered. Only one company is open at a time.

Many owners keep more than one set of books: a trading firm and a proprietor's consultancy, a business in two states with two GST registrations, or the books of a family member's shop. Since version 0.2.0 TallyBook keeps each of these as a separate company, and since 0.1.5 the TallyVault password really encrypts a company's data. This guide covers both, with the limits stated plainly.

When does a business need a separate company?

A company in TallyBook is a complete, separate set of books: its own ledgers, stock, vouchers, financial year and reports. As a rule of thumb:

SituationUsually
A different legal entity (another proprietorship, partnership firm or private limited company)Separate company
A separate GST registration (GSTIN), for example a branch in another stateSeparate company, so GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are prepared per GSTIN
A shop and a godown, or two departments, under the same GSTINOne company, with godowns or cost centres
Practice or training dataA separate company you can delete or ignore

If you are unsure, ask your CA how they want the books split before you start entering.

The company keys

KeyWhat it does
F3Change company: shuts the one you are in and opens another
Alt+F3Company Info: Select, Shut, Create, Alter, Change TallyVault
Ctrl+F3Shut the current company

If you press any of these with a voucher on screen that you have not saved, TallyBook asks before discarding it. When TallyBook starts, it shows Select Company so you choose which books to open.

Create a second company

  1. Press Alt+F3 and choose Create.
  2. Fill in the company name, address, state, financial year and books beginning date. Turn on GST and enter the GSTIN if the business is registered.
  3. Press Ctrl+A to accept. The new company opens with the standard groups, ready for ledgers.
  4. Press F11 to turn on the features this business needs (inventory, payroll, TDS and so on). Features are set per company.

Upgrading from an earlier version? Your existing data opens as the first company. It is not moved or copied, so there is nothing to migrate.

How companies are stored

Each company has its own data file on your computer and its own TallyVault. A backup (Alt+Y → Backup) is taken for the company that is open, so back up each company separately. See backup, restore and data safety.

TallyVault: what it does now

A TallyVault password encrypts the company's data on disk. Without the password the data file is unreadable, even if someone copies it off the computer. Backups of that company are encrypted too. In the desktop app the encryption uses SQLCipher, a widely used encrypted database format.

  1. Open the company and press Alt+K → Company Security.
  2. In the TallyVault section, type the password twice and accept. You can also reach it with Alt+F3 → Change TallyVault.
  3. Shut and reopen the company: TallyBook asks for the TallyVault password first.
  4. Take a fresh backup, so your latest backup is protected by the same password.

The same screen changes or removes the password. Each company's TallyVault is separate, so you can protect only the companies that need it.

A lost TallyVault password cannot be recovered. It never leaves your computer, and there is no reset or back door, so neither you nor we can open the company or its encrypted backups without it. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe, away from the computer.

Most businesses with staff use both. See users, roles and passwords.

Limits today

  • Only one company is open at a time. TallyPrime can load several together; in TallyBook you switch with F3.
  • No group companies (consolidated reports across companies) and no split company yet.
  • TallyBook is single-user: one person works in a company on one computer at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open two companies at the same time?

No. TallyBook opens one company at a time. Press F3 to change company or Alt+F3 for Company Info.

I forgot my TallyVault password. Can it be reset?

No. A lost TallyVault password cannot be recovered by you or by Techardent Labs, and the company and its encrypted backups cannot be opened without it. Keep the password written down somewhere safe.

Are backups encrypted too?

Yes. Backups of a company with a TallyVault password are encrypted. Take a fresh backup after you set or change the password.

I upgraded from an older version. Where is my data?

Your existing data opens as the first company. It is not moved, so nothing needs to be migrated.

Does TallyVault replace users and passwords?

No. TallyVault encrypts the data file and backups; users and roles control what each person at the keyboard may do. Many businesses use both.

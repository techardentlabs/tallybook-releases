TDS under the Income-tax Act, 2025: what changed from 1 April 2026

Techardent Labs · 3 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answer: From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act. TDS on payments to residents moves from the 194 sections to the table in section 393(1) (194C is now Sl. No. 6(i)), salary TDS is section 392, and the quarterly statement Form 26Q becomes Form 140. The earlier of credit or payment decides which Act applies. Since version 0.2.0 TallyBook keeps both sections on every nature of payment and picks one by voucher date.

On 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961. For a business that deducts TDS, the work is the same (deduct, deposit, file quarterly), but the section numbers, the form numbers and some wording have changed. This guide sets out what changed, based on the Act as published in the Gazette and on the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal (sources at the end), and how TallyBook handles both laws side by side.

The new Act in brief

  • The Income-tax Act, 2025 (No. 30 of 2025) received assent on 21 August 2025 and came into force on 1 April 2026 (section 1(3)).
  • It uses "tax year": the twelve months of the financial year starting 1 April (section 3). The year 2026-27 is the first tax year under the new Act.
  • TDS on salary is in section 392. TDS on other payments is in section 393, as tables instead of separate sections: 393(1) for payments to residents, 393(2) for payments to non-residents and 393(3) for payments to any person. TCS is in section 394.

Two definitions in section 402 decide whether you must deduct at all: a "specified person" is anyone other than an individual or HUF, or an individual or HUF whose turnover in the preceding tax year was above ₹1 crore (business) or ₹50 lakh (profession); a "buyer" for purchase of goods is one whose turnover in the preceding tax year was above ₹10 crore. The Finance Act, 2026 also adds "supply of manpower" to the meaning of "work" for contractor TDS. Check your own case with your CA.

The e-filing manuals give the due dates for Forms 138 and 140 as 31 July, 31 October and 31 January for the first three quarters, and 31 May after the year for the last.

The changeover: old or new law?

The Department's TDS compliance FAQ says the Act that applies depends on when the earlier of credit or payment happens. If a bill was credited to the party's account on or before 31 March 2026, the 1961 Act applies, even if you pay in April. If both happen on or after 1 April 2026, the 2025 Act applies. For salary, the FAQ says the payment date decides: March salary paid on 31 March 2026 is under the old Act, April salary paid on 30 April 2026 under the new one.

The same FAQ says corrections for periods under the old Act continue in the old Act's forms and formats. That the January to March 2026 quarter itself is filed in Form 26Q, with Form 140 starting from the first quarter of 2026-27, is stated in the Department's Form 140 FAQ on incometaxindia.gov.in, which we could only see quoted in search results, not open; confirm it with your CA or the TDS return utility before filing.

TCS changes worth knowing

Under section 394(1) as amended by the Finance Act, 2026, TCS on scrap is 2% (it was 1%), and timber and other forest produce is also 2%, from 1 April 2026. The Budget release on PIB described this as rationalising TCS on liquor, scrap and minerals to 2%.

How TallyBook handles both Acts

Since version 0.2.0:

  • Each TDS and TCS nature of payment carries two sections: its 1961 section and its 2025 section with the new rates from 1 April 2026. For example, Payments to Contractors shows 194C before that date and 393(1) Sl. No. 6(i) after it.
  • The section is chosen by the voucher date. Vouchers dated up to 31 March 2026 keep the 1961 sections, so old reports and returns do not change.
  • Scrap and timber TCS use 2% from 1 April 2026.
  • The quarterly TDS report is titled Form 140 for 2026-27, and Form 26Q for earlier periods.
  • If you alter a voucher, TDS Details opens again with the earlier deduction filled in.
Because TallyBook picks the section by voucher date, deduct TDS on the voucher that first credits or pays the party (usually the purchase or expense bill). Then the date TallyBook uses is the date the law looks at.
TallyBook prepares a working summary for the quarterly statement. It does not produce the Form 140 FVU/e-TDS file, and it does not work out late filing fees or interest. File through the official utility or your CA. Review the rates and thresholds on each nature before your first deduction of the year.

Setup, deduction and challans are covered step by step in TDS on payments in TallyBook. Salary TDS by regime is in payroll, salary TDS and gratuity.

Frequently asked questions

What replaced section 194C and the other 194 sections?

From 1 April 2026, TDS on payments to residents is in the table in section 393(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. For example, payments to contractors are Sl. No. 6(i), professional or technical fees Sl. No. 6(iii) and commission Sl. No. 1(ii). Salary is in section 392.

Does Form 140 replace Form 26Q?

Yes. Form 140 is the quarterly statement of TDS on payments to residents other than salary under the new rules. Form 138 replaces 24Q for salary.

Which Act applies to a bill booked in March 2026 and paid in April 2026?

The 1961 Act. The Income Tax Department says the earlier of credit or payment decides, and here the credit was before 1 April 2026.

Do I file the January to March 2026 quarter in Form 140?

It should be a Form 26Q: periods under the 1961 Act stay with the old forms. The e-filing FAQ says this for corrections; for the original statement we saw it only quoted from the Department's Form 140 FAQ, so confirm with your CA or the return utility.

Do I need to change my TDS natures in TallyBook?

No. From version 0.2.0 each nature carries both its 1961 section and its 2025 section with the new rates, and TallyBook picks one by voucher date. Review the rates and thresholds before use.

Can TallyBook create the Form 140 file?

No. It gives a quarterly working summary titled Form 140 for 2026-27. Prepare the statement with the official utility or through your CA.

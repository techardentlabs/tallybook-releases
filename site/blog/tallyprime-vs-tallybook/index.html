TallyPrime vs TallyBook: an honest comparison for 2026

Techardent Labs · 3 November 2026 · 8 min read
Quick answer: TallyPrime is the mature choice for multi-user offices, direct GST filing, connected banking and job work, and costs ₹22,500–₹67,500 + GST plus a yearly renewal. TallyBook is free, runs natively on Windows, Mac and Linux, uses the same keyboard workflow, imports Tally data and now covers multiple companies, TallyVault encryption, manufacturing and batches, but it is new, single-user and prepares returns for upload rather than filing them.

TallyPrime is the accounting software most Indian businesses and accountants grew up with. TallyBook is a new, free alternative that keeps TallyPrime's keyboard-first way of working. This page compares the two side by side, including where TallyPrime is clearly ahead, so you can choose what fits your business.

We make TallyBook, so read this with that in mind. Prices for TallyPrime come from Tally Solutions' own pages (linked at the end) and can change. Check with an authorised Tally partner before you buy.

Where they feel the same

TallyBook deliberately follows the TallyPrime way of working, so a trained Tally operator can start immediately:

  • The Gateway home menu with Masters, Transactions, Utilities and Reports.
  • The same voucher keys: F4 Contra, F5 Payment, F6 Receipt, F7 Journal, F8 Sales, F9 Purchase, Alt+F6 Credit Note, Alt+F5 Debit Note.
  • Alt+G Go To, Alt+C to create a ledger from inside a voucher, Ctrl+A to accept, Esc to go back, F11 features, F12 report configuration, Alt+F1 detailed view.
  • The same groups (Sundry Debtors, Duties & Taxes and so on), bill-by-bill outstandings, and the familiar report layouts.

Choose TallyPrime if…

  • Several staff need to work in the same company at the same time over your office network.
  • You want to file GST returns, generate e-invoices or make bank payments directly from the accounting software.
  • You send material to or receive it from job workers, or need batches and alternate units on manufacturing lines.
  • You need several companies open at the same time, or group (consolidated) companies.
  • You depend on a local Tally partner, TDL customisations, or your CA insists on TallyPrime data.
  • You need a product with a long track record today.

Choose TallyBook if…

  • One person keeps the books on one computer.
  • You work on a Mac or Linux and do not want a Windows virtual machine.
  • You want to stop paying a yearly renewal for features you do not use.
  • You are a student or trainee practising Tally-style accounting at home.
  • You are comfortable using a new product and uploading returns on the GST portal yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is TallyBook free?

Yes. TallyBook has no licence fee and no yearly renewal. TallyPrime Silver costs ₹22,500 + GST one-time and ₹4,500 + GST a year for TSS renewal, according to Tally Solutions' published pricing.

Can TallyBook run on a Mac?

Yes. TallyBook runs natively on macOS 11 or newer, on Apple-chip and Intel Macs. TallyPrime installs only on 64-bit Windows; Mac users need a Windows virtual machine or Tally's cloud access.

Can I move my data from TallyPrime to TallyBook?

Yes. Export masters and vouchers from TallyPrime as XML and import them in TallyBook with Alt+O. Your TallyPrime data is not changed.

Does TallyBook support multiple users on a network?

Not yet. TallyBook is single-user per computer. TallyPrime Gold supports multiple users on a local network.

Can I keep more than one company in TallyBook?

Yes. Each company has its own data file and its own TallyVault. Press F3 to change company, Alt+F3 for Company Info and Ctrl+F3 to shut a company. Only one company is open at a time; TallyPrime can load several together.

Does TallyBook support manufacturing?

Yes. Stock items can have a bill of materials, and the Manufacturing Journal records components, co-products, by-products, scrap and additional costs, with Cost Estimation, Job Costing and Material Requirement reports. Job work is not available yet.

Can I file GST returns directly from TallyBook?

No. TallyBook prepares GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the JSON files; you upload them on the GST portal. TallyPrime with an active TSS can file from inside the app.

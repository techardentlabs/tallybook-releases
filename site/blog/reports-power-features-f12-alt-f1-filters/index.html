Report power features in TallyBook: F12, Alt+F1, Ctrl+F, Alt+F2 and drill-down

Techardent Labs · 3 November 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answer: In TallyBook reports, F12 chooses which columns and rows appear, Alt+F1 switches between condensed and detailed views, Ctrl+F filters rows by text, Alt+F2 changes the period, Enter drills down to the next level and Esc comes back. Everything not on the Gateway is under Display More Reports, or one Alt+G search away.

TallyBook's reports look simple, but a handful of keys turn each one into a working tool: choose the columns, expand the detail, filter to what you need, change the dates and drill all the way down to a voucher. These are the same keys TallyPrime users already have in their fingers. This guide covers each one and maps the reports menu.

F12: configure a report

Press F12, switch options with the toggles, and accept with Ctrl+A. The choices are saved for that report on that computer.

ReportF12 options
Trial Balance and Group SummaryShow opening balances; show transactions (Debit / Credit); show closing balances; hide ledgers and groups with zero balances; show detailed format
Day BookShow cancelled vouchers; show voucher type; show voucher number
Ledger VouchersShow running balance; show voucher type; show voucher number
Cost Centre Summary, Budget Variance, Group InterestHide rows with no transactions, budget or interest; show closing balances where relevant

Many inventory, banking, payroll and statutory reports have their own F12 options too. Look for F12: Configure at the bottom of the button bar on the right.

Alt+F1: detailed and condensed

On the Trial Balance and any Group Summary, the condensed view lists only the top-level groups. Alt+F1 expands every sub-group and ledger in place, indented under its parent, so you can read the whole chart of accounts with balances on one screen. Press it again to collapse. To always open detailed, turn on “Show detailed format” in F12.

Ctrl+F: filter

Press Ctrl+F and a Filter box appears above the report. Type part of a name: “GST” on the Trial Balance shows only GST ledgers; a party name on the Day Book shows only that party's vouchers. In the Day Book and Ledger Vouchers the filter also matches the voucher type, number and date. Enter keeps the filter and returns you to the rows; Esc clears it. If nothing matches, the report says “No rows match the filter”.

Alt+F2: change the period

Press Alt+F2 and enter the from and to dates. The period applies to every report until you change it, so you can open the Trial Balance for a quarter and then drill into ledgers for the same quarter. The Balance Sheet uses the end date, “as at”; outstanding reports use it as “as on”.

Enter: drill down to the source

Drill-down works on most reports: a register opens month by month and then the vouchers in that month; the Day Book and Ledger Vouchers open the voucher itself; Bills Receivable opens the party's ledger. You can alter a voucher you reach this way and accept it; the reports above it update immediately. Cancelled vouchers cannot be altered.

Faster still, press Alt+G (Go To) from anywhere and type a few letters: it lists the common reports, GST returns, create screens, import and export, and every ledger by name.

Frequently asked questions

Does changing the period in one report change it everywhere?

Yes. The period is shared by all reports, as in TallyPrime. The current period is shown at the top right; press Alt+F2 again to change it.

Are my F12 settings saved?

Yes, for each report, on the computer you set them on. They stay until you change them again in F12.

Why does F12 do nothing on the Balance Sheet?

F12, Ctrl+F and Alt+F1 are not available on the Balance Sheet, P&L and Bills Receivable or Payable yet. Drill into a group with Enter and use them in the Group Summary.

How do I jump straight to one ledger?

Press Alt+G and start typing the ledger name. Go To lists every ledger as “Ledger: name” alongside the common reports.

Stock Journal, Delivery Note, Receipt Note and orders: inventory vouchers explained

Techardent Labs · 3 November 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answer: Inventory vouchers change quantities, not money. Stock Journal (Alt+F7) moves or converts stock, Physical Stock (Ctrl+F7) sets the counted quantity, Delivery Note (Alt+F8) sends goods out, Receipt Note (Alt+F9) brings goods in, and Rejection In and Out record returns. Sales Orders (Ctrl+F8) and Purchase Orders (Ctrl+F9) only record commitments: they do not move stock.

Accounting vouchers move money between ledgers. Inventory vouchers move goods: between godowns, out to customers before an invoice, in from suppliers before their bill arrives, or back after a stock count. TallyBook includes the same inventory vouchers as TallyPrime, on the same keys.

Before you start

  • Press F11 and set Maintain inventory to Yes. Without it, the keys below show “Enable inventory in F11 (Features) to use this voucher”.
  • Create stock items (Gateway › Create › Stock Item), and godowns if you keep goods in more than one place. With a single godown, TallyBook does not ask for one.

None of these vouchers posts a ledger entry. If you try to add ledger lines, TallyBook refuses with a message such as “Delivery Note vouchers do not post ledger entries”. Each line has an item, a godown (when you have more than one), a quantity and a rate.

Stock Journal (Alt+F7)

Use it when goods change place or form without a sale or purchase. Every line has a Source / Dest. column: Out for goods leaving, In for goods arriving.

  • Godown transfer: 20 cartons of tea Out from “Shop”, 20 cartons In to “Warehouse”.
  • Repacking or conversion: 50 kg loose sugar Out, 50 packets of 1 kg In.

Transfers appear in Inventory Books › Godown Transfers and Stock Transfer Analysis. For true manufacturing, where components are consumed to make a finished item, use a bill of materials and the Manufacturing Journal instead (Stock Journal also has Ctrl+H Change Mode); see manufacturing with a bill of materials.

Physical Stock (Ctrl+F7)

After counting the shelves, enter each item's actual quantity, not the difference. If the books say 120 and you counted 114, enter 114; TallyBook adjusts the balance from that date. Physical Stock entries are left out of movement analysis so they do not look like sales or purchases.

Delivery Note (Alt+F8) and Receipt Note (Alt+F9)

A Delivery Note records goods sent to a customer before you raise the invoice, for example goods sent on approval. A Receipt Note records goods received from a supplier before their bill arrives. Both need a party (a Sundry Debtor, Sundry Creditor or Cash) and immediately change stock.

Linking notes to invoices. Since version 0.1.5, when the party has pending Delivery or Receipt Notes or Orders, a Sales or Purchase invoice asks for a Tracking No., fills in the pending items and links them. An invoice raised against a Delivery Note or Receipt Note does not change stock a second time. Delivery and Receipt Notes in turn ask for an Order No. Pick the pending note when asked rather than typing the items again.

Rejection In (Ctrl+F6) and Rejection Out (Ctrl+F5)

Use these for goods rejected on quality grounds that come back from a customer (In) or go back to a supplier (Out) before any Credit Note or Debit Note. The same caution applies: a Credit Note or Debit Note with items also moves stock.

Sales Order (Ctrl+F8) and Purchase Order (Ctrl+F9)

Orders record a promise, not a movement. TallyBook leaves them out of every stock balance and valuation. Track them in Gateway › Display More Reports › Inventory Books › Orders and Bills:

  • Sales Orders (Due Only) and Purchase Orders (Due Only)
  • Sales Order Stock Group Outstandings, Sales Order Ledger Outstandings and Purchase Order Ledger Outstandings
  • Sales Bills Pending and Purchase Bills Pending

Checking the result

  1. Press S at the Gateway to open the Stock Summary. Quantities include every inventory voucher except orders.
  2. Press Enter on an item to see its movements.
  3. For godown-wise balances, open Display More Reports › Inventory Books › Godown Summary.
  4. Use Alt+F2 to see stock as on an earlier date.
Batches and price lists work in vouchers too. With Maintain batches on in F11, invoices, delivery and receipt notes, rejections and stock journals ask for batch allocations for batch-wise items. With multiple price levels on, invoices fill the rate and discount from the party's price level. See batches, expiry and price levels.

How stock is valued is explained in FIFO, LIFO and average costing. For the money side of sales and purchases, see accounting voucher types and GST invoices.

Frequently asked questions

Do inventory vouchers affect my Profit & Loss?

Not directly. They post no ledger entries, so they do not touch any account. They change quantities, and through quantities the closing stock value that appears in the P&L and Balance Sheet.

Why does pressing Alt+F7 show an error?

Inventory vouchers need inventory turned on. Press F11 and set Maintain inventory to Yes. Until then TallyBook shows “Enable inventory in F11 (Features) to use this voucher”.

How do I move stock from my shop to my godown?

Use a Stock Journal. Add the item twice: once as Out from the shop, once as In to the godown, with the same quantity. The total stock is unchanged, but each godown's balance moves.

Will a Sales Order reduce my stock?

No. Orders are excluded from every stock balance. They appear in the order reports under Inventory Books › Orders and Bills until you deliver or invoice.
